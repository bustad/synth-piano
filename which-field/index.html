How to edit: a line like [Major triad] starts a group, and every line below it is one variant the page may pick at random. Notes: C4 is middle C; use # or b for accidentals (F#3, Bb3). /2 sets the length in beats (default 1, decimals like /.5 work). + plays notes together (C4+E4+G4/3), a space moves to the next note, and R/1 is a rest. Lines starting with # are comments. Use the group names in the A and B fields (case is ignored).

Edits to the library, starting points and fields are saved automatically in this browser.
