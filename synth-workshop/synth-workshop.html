Synth workshop

A three-oscillator subtractive synth. Click or touch the keys (higher on a key is softer, lower is harder), or use the Z and Q rows of your keyboard. Space sustains, arrow keys shift the octave, and MIDI keyboards work in Chrome and Edge. Changes affect new notes; effects change live.

Signal flow

Grey arrows carry audio. Blue arrows carry control signals, dashed for the LFO. The dashed box marks where pitch is modulated. Dimmed blocks are switched off by the current settings, and values update as you turn the controls.
